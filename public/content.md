## 🏛️ Section 1: Home Page & Philosophy

### Main Statement
- **English**: Spend less time on repetitive work. More time on what actually matters.
- **Hebrew**: האם המשאבים בעסק שלכם מוקצים למקומות הנכונים?

### Sub-headline / Founder Intro
- **English**: "I'm Isaac, and I'm your technical partner for building ultra-fast websites, workflow automations, and AI systems that save you hours of work every week. Instead of selling you marketing slogans, I built interactive tools below to help you diagnose and solve bottlenecks yourself right now. Pick a tool, answer a few questions, and get an accurate assessment in minutes."
- **Hebrew**: "אנחנו משלבים ניסיון עסקי, טכנולוגי ותפעולי כדי לזהות איפה העסק שלכם מאבד זמן, כסף ואנרגיה — ולבנות מערכות מותאמות שמחזירות את המשאבים האלה למקומות שבהם הם מייצרים את הערך הגבוה ביותר."

### "Sites for Now" Philosophy (Why we built this site this way)
In the age of AI, businesses don't need websites just because "businesses need websites." A traditional, static brochure site offers little value when a user can just ask ChatGPT for information. To survive and justify their existence, modern sites must be action-oriented and provide immediate utility. 
Shiiift is built on this "Sites for Now" philosophy: delivering immediate value through interactive diagnostics, audits, and actionable estimates rather than serving as a passive marketing pamphlet.

## 🛠️ Section 2: Interactive Diagnostic Tools

Shiiift hosts a dashboard of self-guided diagnostics. Each tool evaluates a specific business dimension:

### 1. Business Work Maturity Audit
- **Goal**: Identifies manual tasks and estimates annual overhead costs caused by slow processes.
- **Inputs**: Manual work volume, data entry frequency, communication friction.
- **Output**: Numeric maturity score (0–100) and localized annual manual work cost estimate (calculated at $25/hr rate).

### 2. Website Performance Audit
- **Goal**: Evaluates site loading speeds, conversion leaks, and technical optimization requirements.
- **Inputs**: Current platform, perceived speed, mobile usability.
- **Output**: Performance score (0-100) and actionable speed optimization roadmap.

### 3. Automation Finder
- **Goal**: Pinpoints exact workflows ripe for automation.
- **Inputs**: Software used, manual task friction points, sync requirements.
- **Output**: Custom automation pipeline recommendation (e.g. Lead -> WhatsApp -> CRM integration).

### 4. AI Readiness Checklist
- **Goal**: Assesses if a business can actually benefit from custom LLM integrations.
- **Inputs**: Data structure, customer support load, content generation needs.
- **Output**: Personalised AI insight summarizing opportunities and potential bottlenecks.

### 5. Tool Stack Simplifier
- **Goal**: Audits existing subscription software to eliminate redundancies.
- **Inputs**: List of active subscriptions, user seats, overlapping features.
- **Output**: Recommended consolidation strategy to cut software overhead.

### 6. Lead Flow Mapper
- **Goal**: Maps the path from lead generation to close, highlighting leakages.
- **Inputs**: Current acquisition channels, CRM usage, follow-up times.
- **Output**: Pipeline mapping and local CRM recommendations (e.g. HubSpot, Pipedrive, monday.com).
